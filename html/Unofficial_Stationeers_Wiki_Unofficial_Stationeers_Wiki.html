Description[edit]

Wrench is one of the tools you start with.

The MK II version, is resistant to high temperatures, thus really only being useful in hot atmosphere's such as Vulcan and Venus.

Use[edit]

Use left-click while wrench is in active hand to use. The wrench is used for

  • Adjusting the angle and rotation of solar panels.
  • Removing iron sheets from iron frames.
  • Deconstructing pipes.
  • Assembling advanced machines.

Wrench is handy to have it with you at all times during early game stages to make quick adjustments of your solar panels.
