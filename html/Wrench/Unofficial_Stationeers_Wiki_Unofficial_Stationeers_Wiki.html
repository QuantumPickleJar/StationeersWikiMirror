Description

Wrench is one of the tools you start with.

Use

Use left-click while wrench is in active hand to use. Wrench is used on solar panels to adjust them, for taking off sheets from iron frames and to deconstruct pipes.

Wrench is handy to have it with you at all times during early game stages to make quick adjustments of your solar panels.
