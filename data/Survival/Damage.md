Damage is a survival metric. 

 Type of damage 

 Organ damage 
The body of a character in Stationeers consists of multiple organs, different kinds of damage will damage different kinds of organs. For example, fire or toxins will damage the lungs, and as such the oxygen supply to the brain will be restricted, and the brain will eventually start taking damage.

 Kinetic Damage 
The most common problem with kinetic damage is that it will damage the suit of the character, which can restrict the oxygen flow, or subject the character to high heat or pressure from the outside atmosphere.

 Medical Supplies 
There are plans for many ways to heal the characters' organs, as well as eventually replacing individual organs through surgery.
For now, there is a magic Heal Pill item that can be made through Chemistry, it will heal all of the character's organs.

 Cause of damage 
Listing many thing that can cause damage to the player.

 Standing between elevator door while it's closing
 fall from a very high place (also apply on moon despite low gravity) (cause of high place can be because of explosive or massive upward air movement).
 Glitching through terrain while digging
 Excessive heat may cause damage; however, the specific temperature threshold is not specified in available documentation
