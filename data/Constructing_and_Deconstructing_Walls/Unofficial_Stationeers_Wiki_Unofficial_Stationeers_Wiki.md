## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Constructing_and_Deconstructing_Walls/en&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Summary")]

Getting the basic structure for your base is usually the one of the first things you do. You will need a base to install your solar panels, [solid fuel generator](/index.php?title=Solid_fuel_generator&action=edit&redlink=1 "Solid fuel generator \(page does not exist\)") and get the [battery charger](/index.php?title=Battery_charger&action=edit&redlink=1 "Battery charger \(page does not exist\)") hooked up to an [APC](/Power_Control "Power Control") to get power from the [solar panels](/Solar_panels "Solar panels"). You need frames to attach these [structures](/Category:Structures "Category:Structures") too.

### Iron Frames[[edit](/index.php?title=Constructing_and_Deconstructing_Walls/en&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Iron Frames")]

[Iron Frames](/Iron_Frames "Iron Frames") are your basic building blocks. [Iron walls](/index.php?title=Iron_walls&action=edit&redlink=1 "Iron walls \(page does not exist\)") can be used but they don't allow [structures](/Structures "Structures") to be placed. Iron frames do not have to have iron sheets added on them to place structures. You can use the frame itself. The frame without sheets allows you to walk through it and place [cables](/Cables "Cables") and [pipes](/Pipes "Pipes") inside. Which you can seal up after to pass connections through rooms.

## Constructing[[edit](/index.php?title=Constructing_and_Deconstructing_Walls/en&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Constructing")]

Grab [Iron Frames](/Iron_Frames "Iron Frames") into active hand, use right-click to bring up place mode and left-click when frame is in desired spot. 

Then equip a welder in one hand and [Iron Sheets](/Iron_Sheets "Iron Sheets") in the other hand, two per frame you wish to install. The welder needs to be the active hand. 

Use the 'O' button to toggle the welder on. 

[![Welder on.jpg](/images/thumb/8/87/Welder_on.jpg/300px-Welder_on.jpg)](/File:Welder_on.jpg)

Then aim at the frame of the [Iron Frames](/Iron_Frames "Iron Frames") and left click. The welder will go through a green progress bar adding one [Iron Sheet](/Iron_Sheets "Iron Sheets") from the inactive hand. 

[![Left-Click to start Welding](/images/thumb/c/c0/Iron_frames_constuct.jpg/300px-Iron_frames_constuct.jpg)](/File:Iron_frames_constuct.jpg "Left-Click to start Welding") [![Welding Progress](/images/thumb/7/7a/Iron_frames_welding.jpg/300px-Iron_frames_welding.jpg)](/File:Iron_frames_welding.jpg "Welding Progress")

That completes one wall/ceiling/floor that you can place structures on. 

## Deconstructing[[edit](/index.php?title=Constructing_and_Deconstructing_Walls/en&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Deconstructing")]

The frame is broken down in stages. First you remove the sheets using a [wrench](/Wrench "Wrench") and then the [angle grinder](/Angle_Grinder "Angle Grinder") to take down the actual frame once all sheets are removed. 

It is often needed to remove one layer of [Iron Sheets](/Iron_Sheets "Iron Sheets") so you can pass new cables through a wall. In which case on [wrench](/Wrench "Wrench") is needed.
