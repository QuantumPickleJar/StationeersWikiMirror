## Description

[Wrench](/Wrench "Wrench") is one of the [ tools you start with](/Starting_Gear "Starting Gear"). 

## Use

Use left-click while wrench is in active hand to use. Wrench is used on [solar panels](/Solar_panels "Solar panels") to adjust them, for taking off sheets from [iron frames](/index.php?title=Iron_frame&action=edit&redlink=1 "Iron frame \(page does not exist\)") and to deconstruct [pipes](/Pipe "Pipe"). 

_Wrench is handy to have it with you at all times during early game stages to make quick adjustments of your solar panels._
