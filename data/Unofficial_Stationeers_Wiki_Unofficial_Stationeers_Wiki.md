## Data Port

### From Unofficial Stationeers Wiki

##### 

A data port can be connected to the network, which allows control with [Logic I/O](/Kit_\(Logic_I/O\) "Kit \(Logic I/O\)") or programming using the [Integrated Circuit (IC10)](/Integrated_Circuit_\(IC10\) "Integrated Circuit \(IC10\)").
