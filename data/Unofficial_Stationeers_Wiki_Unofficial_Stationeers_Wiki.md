## Description[[edit](/index.php?title=Wrench&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Description")]

**Wrench** is one of the [ tools you start with](/Starting_Gear "Starting Gear"). 

The MK II version, is resistant to high temperatures, thus really only being useful in hot atmosphere's such as [Vulcan](/Worlds#Vulcan "Worlds") and [Venus](/Worlds#Venus "Worlds"). 

## Use[[edit](/index.php?title=Wrench&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Use")]

Use left-click while **wrench** is in active hand to use. The **wrench** is used for 

  * Adjusting the angle and rotation of [solar panels](/Solar_Panel "Solar Panel").
  * Removing [iron sheets](/Iron_Sheets "Iron Sheets") from [iron frames](/Iron_Frames "Iron Frames").
  * Deconstructing [pipes](/Pipe "Pipe").
  * Assembling advanced machines.



_Wrench is handy to have it with you at all times during early game stages to make quick adjustments of your solar panels._
