[[Category:Tutorials]] <languages /> <translate> == Summary == <!--T:1--> Getting the basic structure for your base is usually the one of the first things you do. You will need a base to install your solar panels, [[Solid Fuel Generator|solid fuel generator]] and get the [[Battery Charger|battery charger]] hooked up to an [[Power Controller|APC]] to get power from the [[Solar Panel|solar panels]]. You need frames to attach these [[:Category:Structures|structures]] too. === What you need === <!--T:2--> * [[Iron Frames]] * [[Iron Sheets]] * [[Welder]] * [[Wrench]] * [[Angle Grinder]] === Iron Frames === <!--T:3--> [[Iron Frames|Iron frames]] are your basic building blocks. [[Iron Walls|iron walls]] can be used but they don't allow [[:Category:Structures|structures]] to be placed. [[Iron Frames|Iron frames]] do not have to have iron sheets added on them to place [[:Category:Structures|structures]]. You can use the frame itself. The frame without sheets allows you to walk through it and place [[Cables|cables]] and [[Pipes|pipes]] inside. Which you can seal up after to pass connections through rooms. <!--T:4--> <gallery> Iron frames.jpg|Iron Frames Iron frames state 1.jpg|Placed Iron frames state 2.jpg|One sheet added Iron frames done.jpg| Second sheet added completed </gallery> == Constructing == <!--T:5--> Grab [[Iron Frames|Iron Frames]] into active hand, use right-click to bring up place mode and left-click when frame is in desired spot. <!--T:6--> Then equip a welder in one hand and [[Iron Sheets]] in the other hand, two per frame you wish to install. The welder needs to be the active hand. <!--T:7--> Use the 'O' button to toggle the welder on. <!--T:8--> [[File:Welder on.jpg|300px]] <!--T:9--> Then aim at the frame of the [[Iron Frames]] and left click. The welder will go through a green progress bar adding one [[Iron Sheets|Iron Sheet]] from the inactive hand. <!--T:10--> [[File:Iron frames constuct.jpg|300px|Left-Click to start Welding]] [[File:Iron frames welding.jpg|300px|Welding Progress]] <!--T:11--> That completes one wall/ceiling/floor that you can place structures on. == Deconstructing == <!--T:12--> The frame is broken down in stages. First you remove the sheets using a [[wrench]] and then the [[Angle Grinder|angle grinder]] to take down the actual frame once all sheets are removed. <!--T:13--> It is often needed to remove one layer of [[Iron Sheets]] so you can pass new cables through a wall. In which case only the [[wrench]] is needed. </translate>
