## Description[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Description")]

There are two types of fuel, solid fuel and gas mixtures. 

### Solid fuel[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Solid fuel")]

There are currently only two solid fuel available in the game [Coal](/Special:MyLanguage/Ore_\(Coal\) "Special:MyLanguage/Ore \(Coal\)"), and [Charcoal](/Charcoal "Charcoal"). Both can be used to fuel the [Solid Fuel Generator](/Solid_Fuel_Generator "Solid Fuel Generator"). When burning coal and charcoal they produce the following gases. 

Source  | Pollutant  | CO2   
---|---|---

### Gas Fuel[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Gas Fuel")]

In order to use gases for fuel you need a combustible gas combined with an oxidizer. The only flammable gas in the game is [Volatiles](/Volatiles "Volatiles") (labeled as H2), the two oxygen sources are [Oxygen](/Oxygen "Oxygen") (2:1 mix simply called fuel) and [Nitrous Oxide](/Nitrous_Oxide "Nitrous Oxide") (1:1 mix called superfuel or NOS). This is best done using a [Gas Mixer](/Gas_Mixer "Gas Mixer") \- make sure that both gasses have equal temperature for correct ratio. 

When using liquid fuel some heat energy produced will be spent to evaporate liquid fuel components.

## Using Fuels[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Using Fuels")]

### Fuel Combustion[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Fuel Combustion")]

Autoignition Temperature: 300℃  
2 Volatiles + 1 O2 -> 3 Pollutant + 6 CO2 \+ 572kJ 

### Superfuel Combustion[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Superfuel Combustion")]

Autoignition Temperature: 50℃  
1 Volatiles + 1 N2O -> 2 N2 \+ 2 CO2 \+ 572kJ 

### Fuel Combustion Using a H2 Combustor[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Fuel Combustion Using a H2 Combustor")]

2 Volatiles + 1 O2 -> 2 H2O + 429kJ 

Note that staying below autoignition temperature means condensation for Nitrous Oxide at more than about 1MPa pressure, making it hard to store the fuel mix in large quantities without liquid management. While this reaction releases the same amount of energy as the classic fuel combustion above, it does so using half the volatiles and heating waste gases with less thermal mass resulting in higher temperatures. 

## Collecting gases[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Collecting gases")]

Gases can be collected from a number for sources. On some planets Oxygen may be collected directly from the atmosphere. This can be accomplished with an [active vent](/Active_Vent "Active Vent"), some [pipe](/Pipes "Pipes") and a [gas tank storage](/Gas_Tank_Storage "Gas Tank Storage"). On other planets the oxygen will have to be filtered out from the other gases. A [filtration unit](/Filtration "Filtration") and a [filter](/Filter "Filter") will be needed for this. Some planets, however, have no atmosphere at all. In these cases the gases needed can be obtained by melting or crushing ices. This is by far the most common source for gases early in the game. A basic [Ice Crusher](/Ice_Crusher "Ice Crusher") setup will provide most of your gas collection and mixing needs. 

### Basic Ice Crusher Setup[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Basic Ice Crusher Setup")]

#### Parts required[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Parts required")]

  * 1 x [Ice Crusher](/Ice_Crusher "Ice Crusher")
  * 5 x [Pipes](/Pipes "Pipes")
  * 1 x [Volume Pump](/Pipe_Volume_Pump "Pipe Volume Pump")
  * 1 x [Pressure Regulator](/Pressure_Regulator "Pressure Regulator")



Optional 

  * 1 x [Active Vent](/Active_Vent "Active Vent")



With this setup you can collect gases from ices to mix later. You can also pre-mix the gases by adding Volatile ice and Oxite at a 2:1 ratio. You can then load the pre-mixed fuel in canisters in pre-measured amounts using the pressure regulator. 

#### Setup[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Setup")]

[![](/images/thumb/b/b2/ICE_CRUSHER.png/300px-ICE_CRUSHER.png)](/File:ICE_CRUSHER.png)

[](/File:ICE_CRUSHER.png "Enlarge")

Combination Ice Crusher, Gas collection, and Gas measuring station, Front View (Caution: Be sure to turn of the pressure regulator, and pump al the gas out of the crusher when mixing gases)

  1. Place the ice crusher 
  2. Place a pipe facing to the right
  3. Place a volume pump
  4. Place a pipe tee facing left,right, and down. 
  5. Place a canister storage below the tee
  6. Place a regulator to the right of the tee
  7. Place a tee facing left,right, and down, Or an elbow facing left and down.
  8. Place a second canister storage below the tee or elbow. 
  9. (Optional) Place an active vent to the right of the last canister storage. 



#### Usage[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Usage")]

#### Fuel mixing[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Fuel mixing")]

  1. Place Volatile Ice and Oxites in the ice crusher at a 2:1 ratio.   
***** You can safely fit 8 Volatile to 4 Oxites in a canister. A Ratio of 10:5 will over fill a single canister   
****** Be sure to pump all the gases out of the crusher into a single canister, otherwise the mixture will fluxuate as the grinder processes and outs ices.   
******* Also be sure to turn off your regulator while you are collecting gases.  

  2. Pump the gases to your storage containers.   

  3. Set the regulator to the approximate pressure required to load the amount of fuel you need into the canister.   
***** You can check the exact amount in the canister using the tablet and the gas analyzer 



#### Refiling oxygen tanks[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: Refiling oxygen tanks")]

  1. Purge the pipe network of any spurious gases from previous operations. 
  2. Crush Oxites in the the ice crusher
  3. Pump the Oxites into a canister.   
***** A canister can hold approximately 12 Oxites.  
***** You will need to use a nitrogen filter in additon to your regular CO2 filter when breathing crushed Oxites. 



#### Emptying Canisters[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Emptying Canisters")]

  1. Place any canisters you want to empty into the far left canister storage
  2. Set the active vent to "outward" and turn it on. 



#### Collecting Atmospheric Gases[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Collecting Atmospheric Gases")]

  1. Place any canisters you want to fill in the far left slot. 
  2. Set the active vent to "Inwards" and turn it on.   
***** Be aware that the canister will fill very quickly, be careful not to over-pressurize the canister.



## Mixing Fuel[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=17 "Edit section: Mixing Fuel")]

You can easily mix fuel by adding O2 and H2 to a pipe network in order to put it into canisters or pump into a furnace. 

H2 can be attained by melting the [Ice (Volatiles)](/Ice_\(Volatiles\) "Ice \(Volatiles\)") that can be found in some worlds while being part of the atmosphere of [Vulcan](/Worlds#Vulcan "Worlds"). O2 can be attained by melting the [Ice (Oxite)](/Ice_\(Oxite\) "Ice \(Oxite\)") or again from some atmospheres. N2O is part of [Ice (Nitrice)](/Ice_\(Nitrice\) "Ice \(Nitrice\)") or can be created by the [Nitrolyzer](/Nitrolyzer "Nitrolyzer"). 

Depending on how exact the proportions of your gas mixture are you will end up with different end temperatures and pressures after ignition. 

### Basic Canister Mixing Setup[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=18 "Edit section: Basic Canister Mixing Setup")]

#### Parts required[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=19 "Edit section: Parts required")]

  * 2 x [Portables Connector](/Portables_Connector "Portables Connector")
  * 2 x [Portable Gas Tank](/Portable_Gas_Tank "Portable Gas Tank")
  * 1 x [Pipe Gas Mixer](/Pipe_Gas_Mixer "Pipe Gas Mixer")
  * 1 x [Gas Tank Storage](/Gas_Tank_Storage "Gas Tank Storage")
  * _n_ x [Pipes](/Pipes "Pipes")
  * _n_ x [Cables](/Cables "Cables")
  * _n_ x [Gas Canister](/Gas_Canister "Gas Canister")



#### Setup[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=20 "Edit section: Setup")]

  1. Place both Portable Connectors
  2. Connect both Portable Connectors to the the inputs of the Pipe Gas Mixer
  3. Connect the Pipe Gas Mixer output to the Gas Tank Storage input
  4. Connect the Pipe Gas Mixer power input to power source.



#### Use[[edit](/index.php?title=Fuel&action=edit&section=21 "Edit section: Use")]

  1. Put first Portable Gas Tank or scrubber filled with O2 on one Portables Connector.
  2. Put second Portable Gas Tank or scrubber filled with H2/volatile on the other Portables Connector.
  3. Use a wrench to connect both Portable Gas Tanks to their places.
  4. Optional: for a perfect fuel mix, ensure the temperatures of both gases are exactly the same.
  5. Set the Pipe Gas Mixer to desired combination (34% O2 \+ 66% H2) Note: as the combination is a floating point number, you can also enter 0.333... or 0.666... with a [Labeller](/Labeller "Labeller") to get a more precise mix.
  6. Turn on Pipe Gas Mixer
  7. Place an empty Gas Canister in Gas Tank Storage.
