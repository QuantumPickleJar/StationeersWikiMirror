The Small Tank requires 1 kit and can store 6000L. The Large Tank requires 5 kits and can store 50000L, and is currently the largest capacity container for gasses. Combined with its high bursting pressure (>300 atmospheres) it can store upwards of 72000 mols at room temperature (20 °C).
